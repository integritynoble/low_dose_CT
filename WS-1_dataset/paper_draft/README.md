## v0.5 — why a public-data-only first paper

The two-stage strategy ships v0.5 quickly (~6 months) without waiting on the multi-month Track K + IRB + prospective-acquisition critical path. v0.5's contribution is the **harmonization layer + on-chain anchoring + credential format**, not the underlying scans (which already exist in the cited sources). v1.0 then adds the multi-vendor real-paired-dose contribution as a separate, complementary paper.

Full rationale + dataset-by-dataset gap analysis is in [`../data_needs.md`](../data_needs.md).

## v0.5 — submission notes

*Nature Scientific Data* requires the dataset to be deposited on a stable public repository (PhysioNet here) before submission. For v0.5 the deposit is the **code + manifests + top-up annotations**, not redistributed DICOM; users obtain the underlying scans from each source's gatekeeper and run our preprocessing pipelines locally.

The Data Descriptor format is shorter than a typical research paper: ~3500-5000 words total, with the heaviest weight on Methods + Technical Validation. v0.5 is at the upper end of this range because the harmonization-protocol Methods section is substantive; reviewers tolerate length when the protocol detail is the contribution.

NSD reviewer concerns specific to v0.5:
- **Novelty pushback**: "Is harmonization-of-existing-datasets enough?" Address by emphasizing the four orthogonal contributions (unified loader / on-chain anchoring / harmonized annotations / framework-credentialed baselines) and pointing to v1.0 as the planned follow-up with new data.
- **Vendor-coverage pushback**: "The real paired-dose data is Siemens-only." Address by being explicit in the abstract and limitations; framing v0.5 as the harmonization-layer contribution that v1.0 extends with multi-vendor data.
- **Single-source-annotation pushback**: "Most of your annotations are inherited from LIDC, not new." Address as a feature (harmonizing existing community annotations is the contribution; we top up the gaps rather than re-collect, saving ~$80K in annotation honoraria that would otherwise be wasted).
