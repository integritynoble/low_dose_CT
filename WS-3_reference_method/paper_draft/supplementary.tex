\noindent
This supplement records the configuration needed to regenerate the corpus
bit-for-bit (within IEEE-754 tolerance) from the deposited Apache-2.0
RunBundle. \textbf{Table~S1} lists the reconstruction and training
hyperparameters; \textbf{Table~S2} lists the determinism settings and
pinned software versions; \textbf{Table~S3} discloses training compute.
Entries marked \todo{} are fixed when the Phase-3 models are trained and
the corpus is deposited.
